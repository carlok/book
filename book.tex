\chapter*{Preface}
\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{Preface}

This book is a working collection of thoughts, models, and reflections. Its
center of gravity is mathematical: complex limits, transcendence, dynamical
systems, kinematic structures, morphology, games, and other attempts to make
forms and processes explicit. Around that core there are also essays on
macro-politics and criticism of contemporary Western political culture.

The texts gathered here do not all claim the same archival dignity. Some are
technical notes, some are speculative frameworks, some are essays, and some are
simply attempts to keep an idea from disappearing. The practical aim is modest:
to keep these pieces in one convenient place, organized enough to be useful to
me, and perhaps readable or reusable by someone else.

For now the book should be read as a living draft rather than as a finished
monograph. The chapter copies are the editable book versions; the standalone
article sources remain preserved separately.
